Dragon Spruce BeeMee Schools

EDSIL provided consultancy to the Founders of BeeMee from 2018–2022.

BeeMee nursery and kindergarten at Xiangfu, Changsha opened in Autumn 2020 and a new BeeMee kindergarten opened at Xihu, Changsha in March 2022.

The BeeMee Primary School opened in September 2022, with John as Founding Principal.

EDSIL's support for the Dragon Spruce and BeeMee schools covered architecture, curriculum development, and professional development for this innovative school project in Hunan province.

John is the current Honorary Principal of CBFS.
