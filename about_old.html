About

Mathematician. Educator. School founder.

From the quadrangles of Eton to a top school in Sri Lanka and greenfield sites in India and China. From pure mathematics and philosophy to the practical work of making schools that serve learners a personalised education.

John Puddefoot

John is a mathematician by training and an educator by vocation. His academic work focuses on computational number theory—specifically Carmichael numbers and their higher-order variants, a domain where mathematical beauty meets computational exploration and philosophical depth.

His educational career began at Eton College, where he served as Head of Mathematics and Deputy Head (Curriculum). The move from one of England's most established institutions to founding schools from scratch in India and Armenia was an opportunity to build something new, unconstrained by centuries of tradition.

As Founding Head of The Aga Khan Academy in Hyderabad, John led the project from bare land to IB World School—recruiting staff, designing curriculum, creating institutional culture, and navigating the complex regulatory landscape of Indian education. The experience of building a school shaped his understanding of what matters in education and what doesn't.

Subsequent work in Armenia (with UWC Dilijan) and China (as Honorary Principal of Changsha BeeMee Forest School, of Xuzhou and Yiwu International Academies and adviser to several other schools) reinforced a conviction: the best education starts from learner interests and builds outwards from them, rather than starting from a fixed curriculum and forcing learners to fit into it.

The emergence of AI as a transformative educational technology brings these threads together. John's mathematical background provides fluency with the technology itself; his school-building experience grounds theoretical insights in practical reality; his philosophical bent—particularly a nominalist skepticism about abstract categories—shapes an approach that judges ideas by their effects rather than their pedigree.

Susan Jesudason

Susan brings deep experience in educational administration, school operations, and the practicalities of making ambitious educational visions work day-to-day. Her background spans international education contexts and includes expertise in the operational, regulatory, and pastoral dimensions of school leadership.

The Company

Education Development Solutions International Limited (EDSIL) was established to provide consultancy services to educational institutions—initially focused on school start-ups and international school development, now increasingly centred on the integration of AI into education.

We are based in Norfolk, England, but our work is international. The schools we've helped build serve students from dozens of countries; the questions we're now addressing about AI and education are global in scope.
