This marks a fresh start for the EDSIL blog, now hosted on our new platform.

Over the coming months we'll be sharing reflections on educational leadership, the challenges and rewards of founding international schools, and thoughts on how education can better serve young people in an increasingly complex world. In particular, we'll be looking at **the impact of AI on education**, how to optimise its benefits and how to minimise its risks.

## What to expect

The focus will be on **practical wisdom** based on decades of working with schools across cultures — from Eton to Sri Lanka, from Hyderabad to China. Some posts will be philosophical, others more practical, but all will aim to contribute something useful to the ongoing conversation about what education is for and how we might do it better.

## A note on the archive

The previous blog, hosted on our earlier site, contained posts dating back several years. Rather than migrate everything, we've chosen to start afresh. Those earlier pieces served their purpose at the time; this is an opportunity for new thinking and a fresh start. Some old posts may gradually reappear if we think they retain their relevance.
