About Us

"Educational success lies not only in finding the unique path that each of us can best take through life, but in acquiring the courage, resilience and skills needed if we are to travel down that path in a way that makes a sustainable contribution to human thriving while allowing us a sense of ongoing personal fulfilment."

Meet the team behind EDSIL

We are motivated to help transform education using the extraordinary and unprecedented opportunities afforded by the emergence of AI.

Our mission is to create educational experiences based upon and driven by student interests that inspire and engage them and so enable them to take charge of their own learning. That makes education not only effective but also meaningful by empowering students to thrive in an ever-changing world.
