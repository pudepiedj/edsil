Judge Business Academy, NingXiang

Sir Paul Judge planned to open a Business Academy in co-operation with Ning Xiang International School run by Bright Scholar Education. His untimely and sudden death prevented the project from being completed.

The academy would have offered IGCSEs in year 10 and A Levels in years 11 and 12, taught by a mixture of English-speaking and Chinese teachers.

EDSIL provided advisory support on curriculum development and international partnerships during the planning phase, and John was the Interim Head from May-July 2017.
