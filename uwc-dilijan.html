United World College Dilijan

Founding Head, 2013–2016. Bringing the UWC mission of peace through education to the Caucasus.

John was appointed as the Founding Head in mid-2013 while still in Hyderabad, and supervised the school's adoption as a UWC and the IBDP accreditation process before joining in January 2014.

The College opened in August 2014 offering the IB Diploma programme to 97 students from 60 countries. Another 110 students joined in 2015, bringing the total to students from 85 countries.

As a 100% residential school arranged in four houses, the school became home to students from as far away as Brazil, China, Australia and Canada.

UWC Dilijan embodies the United World College movement's commitment to making education a force to unite peoples, nations and cultures for peace and a sustainable future.
