United Kingdom

Eton College

25 years at one of the UK's most historic and influential educational institutions.

John was appointed to Eton College as an Assistant Master (Mathematics) in 1984.

He became Head of Mathematics in 1993, Director of Studies in 2001 and Deputy Head (Curriculum) in 2002.

He was an Independent Schools Team Inspector from 2000-2009.
